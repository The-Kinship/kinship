Our story

The family you choose

The Grand Companies want your sword for their wars. The Syndicates want your coin for their pockets. We want neither.

The Kinship is a privately funded sanctuary for the displaced, the disillusioned and the exceptionally talented. It stands in the sprawling Embershade Estate, a mansion left behind by the late Maelstrom Lieutenant Embershade, a man who dreamed of a place where the forgotten could finally rest. We bow to no city-state and answer to no official ledger.

Whether you are running from a past you would rather forget, looking to study arts the authorities fear, or simply want a roof and a warm hearth, there is a place for you here.

No need to leave your FC

You do not have to join the free company in game

You can just use the plugin in game.

Our plugin is a self-contained RP system that connects everyone in game, whatever their in-game free company. That makes joining our official FC completely optional. Raiding hard in another free company? Part of a big social guild that does not roleplay? Stay right where you are and join The Kinship in game using just the plugin.

You get the same story, the same RP tools and the same community. The only differences are that plugin-only members do not get a room in the FC mansion, and use linkshells instead of the FC chat. Choose “Just the plugin” on the application.

Beneath the Embershade roof

Four promises

I

The price of admission is nothing

We do not want your gil, your sword or your service. The Harbinger provides this roof, this food and this safety as a gift, not a transaction.

II

Absolute autonomy

Your life is your own. You are free to remain simply Kin. If enemies beat against our gates and you lock yourself in your chamber until the storm passes, so be it. You will not be shamed, and you will not be cast out.

III

The willing shield

Protection is a choice, never a mandate. Those in the Iron Vanguard stand between you and the threat because they chose to, not out of duty or law.

IV

The sanctuary rule

This house asks only one thing in return: respect the sanctuary of others. Violence, theft or betrayal against a member under this roof breaks the peace, and whoever breaks the peace breaks the Kinship.

Your first steps

Everyone enters as Kin

In character, you are granted absolute asylum under the Harbinger’s roof. Your past stays at the door.

You are not expected to fight, to work or to perform. You are given a room, a warm meal and the fierce protection of everyone inside these walls. Stay Kin for as long as you like. When you are ready to lend your talents to the house, you may pledge to one of four sects.

Out of character

How we play

Heavy roleplay and casual socializing, all experience levels welcome. If you just want a chill, social family, you are welcome to stay Kin.

Consent is king

Major developments such as permanent injury, romance, betrayal or death need everyone’s out-of-character consent.

In character stays in character

IC rivalries and arguments never reflect how players feel about each other.

No metagaming or powergaming

Use only what your character would know, and let others react before deciding their outcome.

Rule of cool

We follow the general lore of FFXIV, but we pick collaborative fun over lore-policing.

A safe space for all

Bullying, harassment, hate speech or toxic behavior means immediate removal.

“A house of morals and choices, and we never said they had to be good ones.”
