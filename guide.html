The toolbox

What it is

Rafter Cat's RP Toolbox is a small plugin for Final Fantasy XIV that gives our role-play things to hold, wear, find, send and fight with. Items, gear, profiles, stashes, quests, mail, combat and group play, all in one window beside the game.

It never plays the game for you. It only reads simple things like where you are standing, so a sign knows you are near it. Game NPCs are just triggers for quests, and everything else happens in the plugin's own windows. It is free to use, and everything in it is for RP only.

Items & crafting

RP-only items, stacks, reagents that combine, locked boxes and items that hold items.

Gear & armory

Wear gear, show it off, and watch it break with use.

Profiles

A character card that appears when someone mouses over you.

World of finds

Stashes, signs and pickups you leave in the real world, shown only when you stand near them.

Statuses

Buffs, debuffs, sicknesses and curses with a diagnosis system.

Quests

Story quests that run on their own, with fights and place-based steps.

Combat & duels

Server-rolled dice, shared encounters and player-versus-player fights.

Mail & bank

Letters, parcels and an RP currency with a banker.

Parties

Group RP pop-ups and quests that move together.

Currently in private testing. The plugin is being tested by a small group before it opens to the free company. This guide shows what is coming and how simple it is to use. Would you like to help test it? Join the alpha test.

Why it is different

The story keeps running

Most RP tools only work while the person who made them is online. This one does not. Everything lives on our server, so a storyteller can use a free evening in the middle of the week to write a few quests, hide items around the world, leave stashes and signs, and send mail, then get on with their life.

When the free company logs in at the weekend, the story is already waiting: quests to follow, things to find and letters to read. Some finds even come back on a timer. It is a little like logging into the game and finding your tasks ready for you.

The story keeps running while the storyteller is away.

Under the hood

Built to last

Standard plugins break because they dig deep into FFXIV’s internal engines and UI files. When Square Enix changes a game file, those plugins shatter.

My toolbox views FFXIV from the outside. It treats the game like a backdrop. It uses universal data, like map coordinates and NPC names, which Square Enix never changes. Because the logic runs externally through Cloudflare and Google Sheets, the plugin is decoupled from the game’s core patches.

It wasn’t built to evolve with the game. It was built so the game can evolve around it.

It is built to last through the 1st expansion or the 10th.

First time

Getting started

It takes about a minute. You do not need to learn anything first, since each tab explains itself as you open it.

  1. Install the plugin. Members are given the install address and a short walkthrough when it opens up.
  2. Open it. Click the Kinship entry in the top bar of the game, or use the plugin's command. The main window opens.
  3. Register on the RP tab. Type your character's name and the member password your free company gave you.
  4. Save your recovery code. One code covers all of your characters. Write it down. It is the only way back in if you reinstall or move computers.
  5. Start playing. Each of your characters has its own items, gold and quests, so alts never mix.

Tab by tab

The main window

Click any tab below to open it.

HomeThe front door. Shows the Kinship crest, our tagline and a short welcome, plus the current plot and announcements.

How to use it: Nothing to set up. Open the window and it is the first thing you see.

AboutThe "Start Here" story: who we are, what we offer, how we are led, our sects, an example plot and how to join.

How to use it: Read it top to bottom. It is the same story as the website, inside the game.

RosterEveryone in the Kinship in one place. Sort by sect, rank or A to Z. Each card shows a portrait, race, age, a short bio and a link to their Carrd.

How to use it: Click a person to open their profile. Press Armory on a profile to see what that character is wearing.

AnnouncementsThe same news the website shows, so you never miss a plot update or event.

How to use it: It updates by itself from our shared sheet. Just read.

PlotsWhat is going on in the story right now, and plots that are active for members to join.

How to use it: Open a plot to read the summary and how to get involved.

SectsThe four sects each have a private page for their members.

How to use it: Pick your sect and enter the sect password your leader gave you. Everyone else just sees that the page is locked.

RPThe big one. Everything personal to your character lives here: inventory, gear, profile, stashes, signs, world items, status, bank, mail, quests, fights, combat and parties. Each has its own section below.

How to use it: Register once with your character's name and the member password, and write down your recovery code. After that you are signed in on that computer.

SM ToolsThe control room for story-masters (Advocates) and bankers. Each section asks for its own password, so everyone else simply cannot use it.

How to use it: Only if you run the story or the bank.

GuestFor visitors. Join a combat encounter or a party with only an invite link or code and a name. No registration.

How to use it: Paste the link or code, type a name, join.

SettingsSwitch things on or off: the plot notice, the top bar button, stash pop-ups, name labels in the world, crest size and the login reminder.

How to use it: Tick what you like and untick what you do not.

Inside the RP tab

Everything for your character

Once you are signed in on the RP tab, these are the sections along the top.

InventoryYour RP-only items: not real game items, just things for your story. Each has an icon, a name, a description and flavor text.
  • Make an item, pick an icon, and write what it is. Items stack up to 10 to a slot.
  • An item can say something when used, need a key item before it opens, hold other items inside it, or start a quest.
  • Give items to other players, delete them, or leave them in a stash.
  • Reagents: some items combine into new ones, like three herbs becoming an antidote, with an optional chance of failure.
ArmoryWearable RP gear. Slots for helm, chest, cloak, legs, feet, two rings, a necklace and a weapon.
  • Wear an item from your inventory and it shows in the slot. Other players can look at your gear from the Roster.
  • Gear can carry small attack and heal bonuses (kept to a total cap) that count in combat.
  • Gear can break after a set time worn, so it is not forever.
ProfileYour RP profile: display name, age, race, gender, a portrait link, an "At first glance" line, OOC notes and up to 6 custom fields.
  • Fill in what you want people to see.
  • Other players see it as a card when they mouse over you in the game. It can follow your mouse or sit in a spot you choose.
StashesA hiding place you leave on the map for others to find, or keep for yourself.
  • Stand where you want it, press use this location, name it and put items or gold in it.
  • It only shows up for people standing near it, or for you. It works in houses, apartments and duties.
  • Locked stashes need a key or a word to open.
SignsA message left in the world, like a notice board or a carved warning.
  • Stand somewhere, write the sign and place it. Anyone near it sees it appear as a small window.
  • A sign can also leave gold under it for the first reader.
WorldThings lying around in the world for anyone to pick up, like a frost herb by a tree, or a cookie on a table.
  • Stand where you want it, pick one of your items and name how it looks. Choose how it works: once (first person takes it), wait (it comes back after the minutes you set, like a game respawn) or always.
  • Anyone who walks right up to it gets a window with a Pick up button. They get a copy of the item. Perfect for gathering herbs together for a cure.
StatusBuffs and debuffs for your character, shown on a little bar. Up to 6 at a time, timed or until removed.
  • Items, quests and story-masters can give you one. You can add some for yourself.
  • A status can change your combat rolls, or be a sickness or a curse that someone must diagnose or read before it can be cured.
  • Combat Buff / Combat Debuff are two summary entries that add up every bonus from your statuses, items and worn gear.
BankGold is the RP currency. It is earned in fights and quests, found in the world or paid for jobs.
  • See your balance and history.
  • Bankers can give or take gold with the bank password.
MailLetters and parcels between characters, even when the other person is offline.
  • Send a subject, a message, a short description like "a sealed envelope", gold and up to 3 item stacks.
  • Send it anonymously if you like. Recall it until it is opened. Mail that is not collected returns to the sender after 30 days.
  • A number on the tab shows unread mail.
QuestsStory quests that run by themselves, with no story-master online. Game NPCs are only triggers; everything plays out in plugin windows.
  • Steps can be: talk to someone, hand over an item, receive an item, look, listen or search at a place, walk into an area, or fight.
  • Quests can start by finding a spot, walking into an area, or using an item. Words can change with your gender or a status.
  • Area notices pop up when you walk into a place, whatever step you are on.
  • Good to know for quest writers: NPCs are matched by name only. If several NPCs share a name (guards, chocobo keepers, weather NPCs, Yellow Jackets), the step works on all of them. For one exact spot, use a step tied to a place.
FightPick a fight with another registered player.
  • Challenge someone. They accept or refuse. Both press attack or heal in turns until one wins.
  • The winner gets a "Won a fight" status and the loser "Lost a fight" for 10 minutes.
CombatJoin and run Kinship Combat encounters inside the plugin: classic, last one standing, 1v1 spar and timed.
  • Dice are rolled by the server, so nobody can fudge a roll. Your gear and statuses add their bonuses.
  • An "Encounter over" summary shows the result and any loot.
PartyGroup RP and group quests. Anyone can start a party, share its code and send pop-ups to the whole group or chosen people.
  • Join with the code you were given. Pop-ups appear on your screen and stay off until you join.
  • Group quests: when you are in a party, quest steps are shared. If one person completes a step, everyone in the party on the same quest and step moves on too, rewards included.

Good to know

Handy extras

Top bar button

A "Kinship" entry in the game's top bar. Left click opens the window, right click opens quick options.

Mouse-over cards

Hover over another player to see their RP profile card, with their portrait and what they chose to share.

Status bar

A small movable bar showing your own buffs and debuffs, like the game's own.

Pause button

Not RPing? Press Pause at the top of the window and the plugin stops talking to the server until you turn it back on.

Quiet when idle

If you stand still and leave the mouse alone for a few minutes, the plugin goes quiet and wakes the moment you move.

Report a bug

The ? in the title bar opens About, with links and a form to report a bug or abuse.

Questions

Good questions

Who made this, and is it safe?

This plugin was made with the help of AI (Claude, by Anthropic). The person behind it has no coding skills: they designed the plugin, decided what every feature does and how it works, and directed the work in detail, and the AI wrote the code, helped find and fix bugs, and helped with the rest. That is stated upfront because you deserve to know.

They are not a scammer, a botter or anyone with ill intent. It was made to give our roleplay a fun, immersive tool, and that is all it is.

Left out on purpose:

  • No automation. It never plays the game, presses keys or moves your character.
  • No injecting into or changing the game. It only reads game information, like where you are standing.
  • No connections to other players' computers, and no services like Mare, Universalis or the social plugins.
  • Nothing is downloaded or installed on your PC, and it does not run other programs.
  • No AI inside it. The plugin itself uses no AI service.

Where it connects: only to our own server (the RP system), our website and Google Sheet, and to pictures at links players put on their profiles.

What it sends: your RP name and whatever you put into the RP system, where you are standing when you use something that needs it (stashes, signs, world items, quests), and the name of a player you point at to show their profile card. It does not read your chat, game inventory, gil or account details.

The code is public on GitHub (The-Kinship/kinship-plugin), so anyone can read exactly what it does.

Does it play the game for me?

No. It does not control your character or automate anything. It only reads a little game information, like where you are standing, and shows its own windows.

Do I have to be online for someone to send me something?

No. Mail and gifts wait for you, and quests run on their own. You see them next time you open the plugin.

Are these items real game items, and is RP gold real gil?

No. Items and RP gold only exist inside the plugin, and nothing here touches your real inventory or your Gil. You cannot spend RP gold on anything in the game that costs gil.

But it can be turned into gil. The Kinship offers a 1:1 exchange. Whatever RP gold you earn can be converted into gil, paid to you from the free company vault by a banker.

Can other people see everything about me?

No. Others only see what you put on your profile. Stashes and signs only appear to people standing near them, and your own things are yours alone.

Do I need to be in the Kinship?

Members register with the member password. Visitors can still join a combat encounter or a party with just a link or code, using the Guest tab.

Do I have to leave my current free company to join?

No. The plugin is a self-contained RP system that connects everyone in game, whatever their free company, so joining our official FC is completely optional. You still play FFXIV as normal; the plugin works inside the game. It is great for raiders or members of big social guilds. Everything in the plugin works the same for you. The only differences are that plugin-only members do not get a room in the FC mansion, and you will use linkshells for chat instead of the FC chat.
