Who we are

Quality RP over quantity

The Kinship is a heavy RP free company that focuses on giving its members an in-depth, immersive, and inclusive RP environment to thrive in. We value quality RP over quantity of members.

Though we follow a very classic “Misfit & Kingdom” style, we try to make sure our RP surpasses the standard, dull, repetitive RP tropes that seem to be everywhere.

No need to leave your FC

You do not have to join the free company in game

You can just use the plugin in game.

Our plugin is a self-contained RP system that connects everyone in game, whatever their in-game free company. That makes joining our official FC completely optional. Raiding hard in another free company? Part of a big social guild that does not roleplay? Stay right where you are and join The Kinship in game using just the plugin.

You get the same story, the same RP tools and the same community. The only differences are that plugin-only members do not get a room in the FC mansion, and use linkshells instead of the FC chat. Choose “Just the plugin” on the application.

Find your calling

The four sects

The Onyx Blood Guard

Sect Leader: The Prefect

Our magic section. It handles the research, study, and employment of all magical aspects for the FC.

Great forSummoners, mages, warlocks, anyone interested in the occult, RPs with heavy magic.

It also houses our darker, secret, underground taboo RP place that isn't public.

Example RPs
Ward castingMagical researchRitualsCharm creations

The Iron Vanguard

Sect Leader: The Vanguard Commander

Our military sect and peacekeepers. Heavy RP combat.

Great forAnyone RPing a fighter, guard, or knight, or anyone whose main RP style is fighting.

Example RPs
EscortsPatrolsGuardingSparring

The Life Watch

Sect Leader: The Life Warden

Our healing sect, for those whose main focus is the health and well-being of not only our members' bodies, but their minds as well.

Great forHealers and combat medics. Healers don't stop at the physical, but can also help with mental health as well.

Example RPs
Medical screeningsClinicsResource gathering & potion makingHealing after combat, like a patrol or a sparring session

The Ember Court

Sect Leader: The Baron / Baroness

Strictly our social sect, designed to help push RP plots along, start and engage with new members in casual RP, and help new members find a sect to join. They plan events and social RPs: holiday events, birthdays, classic venue nights.

Example RPs
Planning a Halloween costume partyHosting a venue nightMeeting new members for casual social RP

Each sect plays a vital part in the overall RP of our free company, and each has a special place in our overall RP plots. This helps to ensure everyone is included in some way!
